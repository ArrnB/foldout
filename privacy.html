Foldout privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 (camera section added)

Foldout is a document scanner. It is available as a web page and as an Android app. This policy covers both.

The short version

Foldout does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything it does happens on your device.

Photos and scans

  • Photos you take or choose are processed only on your device: finding the page, straightening it, filters, watermarks and making the PDF or images.
  • The pages you are working on are kept in your browser's or the app's own storage on your device, so a scan is not lost if the app closes. Tapping New deletes them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them too.
  • Files you save go to your device's Downloads folder (Downloads/Foldout in the app). Foldout does not keep copies anywhere else.

Camera

When you tap Take Photo, Foldout asks to use the camera so it can show a live viewfinder that finds the page and can take the photo for you. The camera is on only while that screen is open. Frames are looked at on your device to find the page and are never saved, except the one photo you take, and never sent anywhere. You can refuse; Foldout then lets you choose a photo instead.

What Foldout does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No uploads. The Android app does not even have permission to use the internet.
  • No access to your other photos. Photos are chosen through Android's own photo picker, and Foldout only sees the one you pick.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when the page is loaded, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Foldout itself sends nothing after the page has loaded.

Children

Foldout collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
